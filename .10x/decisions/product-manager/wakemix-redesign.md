# Product manager — Wakemix redesign (2026-10-29)

## Users
People who sleep through alarms or snooze a lot, starting with the testers of the web version.

## Stories
- As a sleeper, I see at a glance when my next alarm rings and what it will play: the record (2.2a, made real in 2.5a).
- I set the time with one thumb, half asleep: the rotary dial, with keyboard entry for everyone else (2.2a).
- I know when to go to bed to sleep 8 hours: "Bed by 22:30 for 8 h of sleep" (2.2a). Later a gentle reminder at that time (2.4a).
- Before sleeping, I can hear tonight's mix, reshuffle it, or leave a sound out for one night (2.5a).
- After Stop, I tell the app which sound woke me, and it learns (2.5b, then Insights in 2.8).

## Scope now
2.2a (the look, the name, the sleep calculator) and 2.3 (the placeholder library), in parallel.

## Later
2.4a, 2.5a and 2.5b, in that order and after the tasks they depend on.

## Not now
- Name that sound (not chosen).
- The app icon (8.1).
- Renaming app IDs and repos.

## Success criteria
- The "Done when" of the spec.
- The user approves the screenshots of 2.2a before merging.
