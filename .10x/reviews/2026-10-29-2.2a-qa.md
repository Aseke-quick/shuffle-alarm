## Findings
1. **Medium, fixed: a selected chip's label failed the contrast rule.** `chipTheme.labelStyle` had the plain text color, and a `FilterChip` uses that style in every state. On the accent fill of a selected chip, that was 2.88:1 in dark and 3.80:1 in light, under 4.5:1. The token test passed because the tokens are right; only the theme paired them wrongly. On branch 2.2a no chip showed, because the library had no genres; the genres of 2.3 are the first chips. The label color is now a `WidgetStateColor`: `onAccent` when selected, as on day keys. New test: "writes a chip label in the text color of its fill", in both themes.
2. **Medium, fixed: after a tap on the dial's ring, its next drag could turn into a scroll.** The dial's own recognizer joins the gesture arena for a pointer that goes down on the ring. On a tap, the list around it gives up on pointer up, and the arena then gives the pointer to the dial, after the dial had stopped tracking it. `_accepted` stayed true, so the next drag skipped the dial's claim on the arena: the list took it after 18 px. In a test, a turn from :15 to :20 stopped at 6:16. `acceptGesture` now does nothing once the pointer is gone. New test: "in a list after a tap on the ring still takes the next drag".
3. **Low: the switch thumb still slides for 200 ms when less motion is asked for.** Known (agent A's tech debt): Material's `Switch` cannot turn it off. A switch of our own in `lib/design/` would.
4. **Low: CanvasKit comes from `www.gstatic.com`.** A request to Google at each first visit, and the app does not start where that host is blocked. Decided in the merge session; see docs/DECISIONS.md.
5. **Low: glyphs no bundled font has** (an emoji, Chinese or Arabic in a label) still make Flutter's web engine download a Noto font from `fonts.gstatic.com`. docs/DECISIONS.md 2.2a records it; the native apps never download a font.
6. **Info: unselected chips and day keys differ from the page by their fill only** (1.18:1 in dark, 1.08:1 in light). Their labels say what they are, which WCAG 1.4.11 allows, and a selected one has the accent fill (4.40:1 or more against an unselected one) and, on chips, a check mark.
7. **Info: an alarm that is off shows its time in `textDisabled`** (2.66:1 in dark). The spec exempts it, and the word "Off" next to it is in `textMuted` (5.73:1), so the state stays readable.
8. **Info: the tabs tell screen readers "button, selected"**, not "tab, 1 of 3". Known (agent A's note).
